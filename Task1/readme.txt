🧠 What I Learned Yesterday
HTML Headings: <h1> to <h6> for organizing content by importance.

Basic Tags & Elements:

<p> for paragraphs

<strong> and <em> for bold and italic text

<ul> and <ol> for lists

<sub> and <sup> for scientific notation (e.g., CO<sub>2</sub>, E = mc<sup>2</sup>)

<hr> for horizontal lines

<br> for line breaks
