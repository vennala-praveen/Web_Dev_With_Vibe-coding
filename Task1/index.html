How to Make a Sandwich

Why I Love Sandwiches

Sandwiches are the perfect blend of simplicity and flavor. Whether you're in a rush or craving something comforting, they offer endless possibilities.
I love how you can make them classic or get creative with ingredients. Plus, they're easy to share and always satisfying!

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 1 tablespoon of mayonnaise
  • 2 slices of cheese
  • 2 slices of tomato
  • Lettuce leaves
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Spread mayonnaise evenly on one side of each bread slice.
  2. Place cheese slices on one slice of bread.
  3. Add tomato slices and lettuce on top of the cheese.
  4. Sprinkle a pinch of salt and pepper.
  5. Cover with the second slice of bread, mayo side down.
  6. Cut the sandwich diagonally and serve fresh!

Tip: Toast the bread lightly for extra crunch!
